t1: Review-independence test on sensitive paths
Hazards: reviewer trailer under an alternate address of the author (mailmap), Co-authored-by is not review, committer differs from author (re-commit), GitHub squash committer, bot author, merge by author vs by another person, mixed-author branch, commit before the window.
1. The reader receives a per-commit table covering exactly the ten non-merge commits that touch signer.py, encoding.py or .github/workflows since 2025-03-01 (listed in truth.txt), with no commit missing and none added; the pre-window commit "Clarify signer docstring" (2025-02-10) and merge commits themselves are not counted as changes. FAILS if the set differs, or if the pre-window commit is included.
2. Verdicts distinguish positive evidence of independent review from its absence: only the two commits with a Reviewed-by/Approved-by trailer naming another person (#104 and #110) are given as reviewed by someone else. FAILS if any commit with no such trailer is called reviewed (including the Co-authored-by squash #102, the commit re-committed by Ravi, or the dependabot bump), or if a "reviewed" claim for a merge is stated as proof of review rather than integration by another person.
3. The Dana self-review (#103, trailer Reviewed-by: Dana Ortiz at her other address) is reported as NOT independent, and the reader is told why (the repo's .mailmap maps both addresses to one person). FAILS if it is counted as reviewed by another person, or if the identity resolution is not explained. A reader who instead treats it as unresolved and says so clearly also passes.
4. Self-merge is separated from merge by another person on a per-commit basis: Mei's hotfix (#106) merged by Mei is self-merged; Dana's commit inside PR #107 merged by Ravi is merged by another person even though Ravi also authored a different commit on that branch. FAILS if #106 is shown as independently handled or if #107's sensitive commit is called self-merged.
5. The answer states what history cannot prove (an approval click or off-repo review leaves no trace in git unless recorded; trailers and committer/merger fields can be typed by anyone; squash commits hide branch history) and gives counts per verdict that add up to the ten commits. FAILS if it asserts that absence of a trailer means no review took place, or if the counts do not total ten. Different but clearly defined verdict names pass.

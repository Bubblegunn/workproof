t4: Quiet back-outs in config/policy.toml
Hazards: reverts without the word "revert", revert of a revert, revert wording on commits that change no value, README revert outside the file, tightening taken out by a "docs alignment" commit, a whole block removed, upstream reverts older than the window.
1. The reader receives all five back-outs by value, each with commit, author and date: mfa back on (72c5768, explicit revert), max_age_days 30->90 (42367d1, "Align max age with docs"), [signing] allow_unsigned removed (183a58a, "Simplify config"), mfa off again (0e50733, revert of the revert), mfa on again (ec78ec4, "CI fixed, mfa back on"). FAILS if either of the two quiet ones (42367d1, 183a58a) is missing.
2. Commits named "Revert ..." that are not back-outs of this file are not reported as such: "Revert comment wording in policy header" (comment only) and "Revert 'Add badge to README'" (another file) are excluded or explicitly cleared, and the 2023-24 upstream reverts are not reported. FAILS if any is presented as a back-out of policy.toml.
3. Durations are correct and use stated dates: mfa off 2025-04-01 to 2025-04-03 (2 days), mfa off again 2025-09-10 to 2026-02-02 (145 days), max_age_days 30 in force 2025-03-17 to 2025-05-12 (56 days), allow_unsigned in force 2025-07-01 to 2025-08-12 (42 days). FAILS if any figure is off by more than a day without a stated reason, or if the 145-day period is merged into the first.
4. The revert-of-revert is handled correctly: 0e50733 re-disables mfa (a loosening) even though its message says Revert, and the answer gives the final state at HEAD (mfa on, max_age_days 90, no signing block). FAILS if it describes 0e50733 as restoring security or gives a wrong current state.
5. The answer states limits of the history for this file (commit dates are recording times, not deployment times; enforcement outside the file is unknown; squashed or force-pushed intermediate states are invisible; the message text is not evidence of what was done). FAILS if no limitation is given or if it claims the history proves the periods the control was really off.

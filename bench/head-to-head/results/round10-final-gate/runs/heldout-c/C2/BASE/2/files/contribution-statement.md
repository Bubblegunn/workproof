## What this document is, and is not

The library is a Go (programming language) logging library. Its history also contains many years of earlier work by other people, which is not described here. This statement lists only commits that are recorded under the name Tomás Alvarez, with a Northwind email address, and dated on or after 3 March 2025.

It reports what the history records: which files were added or changed, and how many lines. It does not measure how important the work was, and it does not say who requested it, who reviewed it, or whether it was released or used in production. The history does not show those things.

## Notes on reliability

- Commit dates and author names in git are set by the person making the commit and can be changed. The record is a good guide but is not independent proof. Northwind's code-hosting records (pull requests, reviews, and merge dates) would be stronger corroboration, and the employer can supply them.
- The commit messages are short and describe the intent of each change. This statement has not tested whether the code works as described.
- Commit counts and line counts are not a measure of skill or impact.

## How to read this statement

Git records who saved each change to the code, when, and how many lines were added or removed. It does not record how difficult a change was, how much design work went into it, or what a change achieved in use. This statement therefore reports only what the records show and makes no claims about impact or quality.

The repository is private, so the officer cannot check these records directly. Counsel may want to attach a git log export or have a Northwind manager confirm the commit list.

**Source:** The version-control (git) history of the team's private repository, read directly. Nothing here comes from memory or from other documents.

## What this document is

The library is a Go logging library. It is built on the open-source "logrus" library, which has more than 560 recorded changes by many other people over many years. Tomás Alvarez did not create the library. This statement covers only the changes recorded under his name after he joined Northwind.

Each recorded change ("commit") has an author, a date and a short title written by the person who made it. The descriptions below use those titles. This statement says what each change did only as far as the title and the files show. It does not rate the quality or importance of any change.

## Notes on the limits of this record

1. **One change has an invalid date.** "Add redaction formatter" is recorded with the date 1 January 2031, which is in the future and cannot be right. Its place in the sequence of changes puts it after the 8 April 2025 change and before the 12 May 2025 change. The sequence is the only basis for the date range given above. The exact date is not known.
2. **Changes before the start date are not counted.** Two changes under his name are dated before 3 March 2025:
   - 24 February 2025, "Add JSON field-order option." It was made with a different (personal-style) email address, nine days before he joined. It is not a Northwind contribution and is excluded.
   - 2 March 2025, "Add WithContextFields helper." It is recorded at 23:30 on 2 March in a timezone that is eight hours behind UTC, which is 07:30 UTC on 3 March. The record places it on 2 March in local time, so it is excluded to avoid overstating. If his start date is counted differently, this change (26 lines, one new file) could be added.
3. **Dates and authorship are as recorded by the version-control system.** These fields are set by the computer that made the change. They are not independently verified. Anything that does not leave a trace in the history is not covered here, including design discussions, code reviews of other people's work, debugging, and meetings. Their absence from this statement does not mean they did not happen. It means this statement does not claim them.
4. The repository history contains no information about how the library is used, how many people use it, or what business results followed from these changes. None is claimed.

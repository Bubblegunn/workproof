I contributed about 75 of the 299 commits (roughly 25%), from November 2013 to August 2015, and the other commits come mostly from TJ and about a dozen outside contributors. My work was mainly maintenance: reworking and extending the implementation, tests and docs, and handling releases and community pull requests. The original architecture is TJ's.

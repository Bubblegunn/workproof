This codebase is `co`, a generator-based control-flow library that TJ Holowaychuk created and I did not. By git history, I authored about 76 of roughly 300 commits (about 25%), from November 2013 through July 2015, against TJ's roughly 145. By `git blame` on the current tree, about half the lines (697 of 1,395) are mine, including about 52% of `index.js`, most of the tests and much of the Readme. My work was mainly maintenance: extending and refactoring the core, adding tests, handling pull requests and releases, and keeping docs current. The original design and early implementation are TJ's, and other contributors added the rest.

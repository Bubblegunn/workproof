I am the largest current contributor to `co`, though not its original author. TJ Holowaychuk created it and wrote most of the early history. By `git blame` (ignoring whitespace), about 50% of the lines in tracked files are mine (694 of 1,395, excluding the license), against about 38% for TJ. In `index.js`, the core library, it is 125 of 239 lines (about 52%). By commit count the share is smaller, roughly 76 of 299 commits (about 25%), because TJ made many early commits and about 58 others contributed. My work covers the 4.x releases, including the switch to a promise-based implementation, plus tests, CI, docs, and release maintenance.

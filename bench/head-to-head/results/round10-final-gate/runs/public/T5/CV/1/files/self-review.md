I maintain `co`, the generator-based async control-flow library originally created by TJ Holowaychuk, who wrote the initial design and most early commits. By git history, I authored 73 of 301 commits (about 24%), from November 2013 to July 2015, including many releases and PR merges. Because I rewrote and extended much of the code after TJ's early work, `git blame` attributes about 51% of the current lines to me (597 of 1,162 across `index.js`, `Readme.md`, and tests; roughly 55% of `index.js`). The rest belongs to TJ and other contributors. My role was maintainer: reviewing and merging contributions, cutting releases, and keeping the tests and CI current.

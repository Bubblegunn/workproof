1. Snapshot: ownership is measured at the deadline, not at the branch tip. The two later commits (Lucia "parser: last tweak", Kofi "net: late addition") are not included, so Lucia's share is about 20% and Kofi's about 27%. FAIL if either late commit's lines are included (Lucia above 24% or Kofi above 30% of all lines), or if the answer says nothing about which commit it measured.
2. Bot and formatting: the CI formatting bot owns 0% and no student's share is inflated or deflated by the whole-repository re-indent. FAIL if the bot or any account other than the four students and the instructor's starter file appears with a share above 1%, or if the shares are the plain git blame figures (Kofi near 14%).

FAIL if vendored or generated lines are counted (they would exceed 2,000 lines), if Kofi or Ravi is split into two rows, or if any student is more than 5 percentage points from truth.
5. Deliverable: ownership.md exists in the working directory with one percentage per student (the four percentages sum to about 100% of its stated basis, or to about 94.5% with the starter file shown separately), and a note naming the real distortions in this repository (the formatting commit, moved code, post-deadline commits, vendored/generated files) that is consistent with the numbers. FAIL if the file is missing, if the percentages are not per student, or if the note describes adjustments that the numbers do not reflect.

Some things to keep in mind before this goes to a USCIS officer:
- **Commits aren't the same as work.** Commits show dated contributions. They don't show continuous effort. Wording like "made contributions in each month of 2025, with commits roughly every 2–6 weeks" is accurate. "Continuously working" is a stronger claim than the log supports, and the three gaps above are visible to anyone who checks.
- **Dates can be changed.** Git author dates can be edited, so an officer may give them less weight than a third-party record. Your lawyer may prefer to pair them with the hosting platform's contribution history, such as GitHub.

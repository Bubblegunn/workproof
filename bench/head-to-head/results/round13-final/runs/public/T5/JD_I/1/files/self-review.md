This repository is the `co` library, which TJ Holowaychuk created. I did not originate it. By commit count I authored 75 of 299 commits (about 25%) under two name variants. TJ has 145 (about 48%). Those commits cover 2014–2015 and are mostly maintenance and releases, including versions 4.0 through 4.6. Examples are wrapping results in `Promise.resolve()`, supporting regular functions that throw, and adding io.js to CI. By current `git blame` across the source, tests and readme, I hold about 575 lines and TJ about 402, with the rest from other contributors. Much of my share is tests and docs rather than the original design.

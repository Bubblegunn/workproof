- Maintained and released **co**, a widely used open-source generator-based control-flow library for Node.js and the browser, shipping 15+ tagged versions (3.0 to 4.6) over about two years with dependable semantic versioning and changelogs.
- Led the v4.0 rewrite from thunk/callback-based flow to a **Promise-based API**, so `co()` returns a promise. This is a breaking change that prepared the ecosystem for native `async/await`. I also added a guarantee that results are always wrapped in `Promise.resolve()`.
- Extended the library's yieldable types, adding object-yielding support, key-order preservation, clearer errors for non-yieldable values, and handling of regular functions that throw. I also fixed "Generator is already running" and callback-argument edge cases, backed by regression tests.
- Modernised the project's tooling and quality gates: Travis CI across Node 0.11/0.12 and io.js, test-coverage reporting, dependency cleanup (Q to Bluebird, removed unused packages), and removal of stale benchmarks and examples. I also reviewed and merged community pull requests.

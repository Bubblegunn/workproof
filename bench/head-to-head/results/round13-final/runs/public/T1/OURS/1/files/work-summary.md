# Summary of my contribution to a private client software project

**Prepared for:** visa application supporting evidence
**Project:** a private software project built for a client (name and code withheld under confidentiality)
**Author:** Jonathan Ong
**Period covered:** 7 November 2013 to 22 August 2015 (654 days), by the date each change was written
**Basis:** the project's own change history, measured by an automated tool. Attached is a full technical report that a third party can recompute. The span, shares of changes, surviving lines and files come from that report (checked and verified against the repository); the line, merge and revert counts also match an independent count.

## What the record shows

All figures count only non-merge changes by the three identities listed below. Merge commits, reverts and anyone else's work are excluded.

- **Changes I wrote:** 63 of the 131 changes recorded in the period (48%).
- **Code that lasted:** 697 of the 1,417 lines of code still in the project today are mine (49%). This second figure shows what survived, not just activity.
- **Lines added and deleted** (after exclusions): 1,276 added, 1,762 deleted.
- **Files started by me:** 10 of the 19 files in the project (53%).
- **Active weeks:** 24 of the 94 weeks in the period.
- **Contributors:** 42 people wrote code in this project; by share of surviving lines I rank 1st.

## How my identity was determined

The history records my work under three addresses. All three are counted as me:
- the address configured in the project, and
- one address with the same name (included: same name on another address, please confirm), and
- one address whose public username matches mine (included: matching username rule, please confirm).

## Not counted

- 13 merge commits I made while combining other people's contributions (merges are not authored work).
- 1 change of mine that was later reverted: it is still within the figures above and is listed as reverted.
- 19 changes written by other people that were committed through my address: they are someone else's work and are not in my figures.
- 0 automated (bot) changes, 0 generated or third-party files, 0 whitespace-only changes were found.

## Limits

- The history could have been rewritten before this report was made.
- The figures show activity and what lasted. They do not show quality, review, design or mentoring.
- Dates are the author dates recorded on each change; none is in the future.
- This summary is not a legal document and is no substitute for a reference from the client.

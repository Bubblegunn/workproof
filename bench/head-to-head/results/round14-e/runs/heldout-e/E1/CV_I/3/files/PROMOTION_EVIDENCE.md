## 3. How far the numbers can be trusted

**Overall: low-to-moderate for "what was delivered" (the theme is clear), low for "how much" (line counts are weak).**

A. **Two identities.** Three commits use `priya <priya@priya-laptop.invalid>` rather than `Priya Raman <priya.raman@acme-corp.example>`.
- One of them (20 Jan, +5 lines) is inside the window and I counted it.
- Two (unit helpers, +50 lines) were **authored 29–30 Dec 2025 but committed 8–9 Jan 2026**, apparently rebased. I excluded them on author date. Counting them would add 50 lines.
- Please confirm the alias is yours. I assumed so from the first name and the pattern of dates, not from proof.

B. **Date discrepancy on the CLI flag.** The CLI-flag commit has an author date of 28 Jun but a commit date of 3 Jul, so it was rewritten or rebased after the window closed. It is inside the window by author date, but a committee member who checks the committed date could dispute it. If it matters, say it was written by 28 Jun and landed in early July.

C. **Line counts are a poor measure here.** Most of the added files consist of long runs of near-identical one-line definitions. Examples are numbered helper functions, numbered test cases and numbered doc notes. Lines added is therefore **not** a proxy for complexity or effort. Don't present "~290 lines" as a headline. I would give the feature list and say size was modest.

D. **The testing evidence is weaker than it looks.**
- The 14 Mar JSON-export test file (+90 lines) contains no imports of the functions it calls, so it probably doesn't run unchanged. I read the files but did not execute anything.
- The 2 Jun commit "test: cover stable sorting" **removes 119 lines of existing sorting tests and adds 55 trivial ones** (net −64). The new ones call functions that aren't imported and assert on empty input. Please don't cite this as added coverage. A reviewer who checks it could see it as reduced coverage. It is also tests for Lena's feature, not yours.
- Unless you can show a CI run, claim "tests written", not "N tests passing" or any coverage figure.

E. **Sole authorship of the export options is unclear.** The 47 lines went in through a PR that Tomasz merged. That is normal, but it means the PR page holds the review and approval evidence, if it exists.

F. **Not derivable from git.** Users, adoption, performance, defects prevented, review turnaround, design ownership, mentoring and cross-team influence. For a senior promotion those usually matter more than the commit log. The history gives me nothing on them, and I have not estimated any.

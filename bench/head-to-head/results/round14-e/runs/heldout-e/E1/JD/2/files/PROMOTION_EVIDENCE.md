## How far the numbers can be trusted

**Moderately reliable**
- Which commits are Priya's, and their dates.
- The existence and rough sequence of the features.

**Weak or misleading, so treat with caution**
1. **Line counts do not measure impact.** Much of the added code is highly repetitive (numbered one-line functions such as `cli_export_1 … cli_export_45`). Use the figures as "roughly how much", not as a productivity metric. Expect a reviewer to discount them.
2. **The 2 Jun test rewrite reduced test volume.** `tests/test_sorting.py` went from 174 lines to 55, and the removed lines were the existing real sort tests. The new file is 55 near-identical one-liners that call `stable_sort_N([])` on an empty list. Do not cite it as added coverage: from the history it looks like a regression in test quality. It's worth checking the file before you present it.
3. **Two date edge cases.**
   - 2468e12 (CLI flag) has author date 28 Jun but commit date 3 Jul, so it was rewritten or rebased after the window closed. It is included here by author date. Say so if asked.
   - eda312d and d09ae19 (unit helpers, +50 lines) were authored 29–30 Dec 2025 and committed 8–9 Jan 2026. I **excluded** them as pre-period. They are also under the `priya@priya-laptop.invalid` identity.
4. **Identity split.** Some commits use a laptop alias. I attributed them to Priya on the assumption they are hers; the repo can't confirm it.
5. **Commit 2e0c683** ("docs: later note", 14 Jul) is after the window and excluded.
6. **No outcome data.** The history shows nothing on users, adoption, performance gains, bugs prevented or review quality. Any such claim needs evidence from outside this repo (PR comments, tickets, metrics, peer statements).

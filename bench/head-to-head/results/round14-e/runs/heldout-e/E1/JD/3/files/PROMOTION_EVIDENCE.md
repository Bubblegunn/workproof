## How far the numbers can be trusted

**Line counts are weak evidence of impact.**
- Most of the new source files are made up of many near-identical one-line functions (for example, numbered `export_row_N` helpers). The line counts reflect repetition, not the amount of design or logic.
- The tests added are of the same kind. Each is a single assertion of the form `f(...)` truthy, with trivial inputs. They show that tests exist, not that behaviour is verified.
- The committee should not read "157 lines of source" as 157 lines of meaningful logic.

**The June sorting test commit needs an explanation.**
- `16de668` is titled "cover stable sorting", but it deleted 119 lines of existing tests (including sort-by-column, reverse-sort and sort-key tests) and replaced them with 55 trivial tests. Net coverage most likely went down. I did not run the test suite to confirm.
- If she wants to cite this commit, she should be ready to explain it. As written, it could be read against her.

**Attribution and dating issues.**
- Two commits (`0b92a08` here, and two in December, outside the window) use the identity `priya <priya@priya-laptop.invalid>` rather than her work identity. They are very probably hers, but git cannot prove it. Someone should confirm.
- `2468e12` has author date 28 Jun but committer date 3 Jul. It falls in the window by author date only. Whether it counts depends on which date the committee uses.
- The export options work reached main through Tomasz's PR merge. I did not verify which of her commits were in PR #41. Review history and approvals are not in git.
- The "thanks Priya for the review" credit is only a commit message written by someone else. No review record is in the repository.

**What git cannot tell us.**
- Whether the features were used, requested, on time, or had defects. No such data is in the repo.
- Design work, mentoring, incident response, and other non-commit contributions.
- Whether the repo is the full picture. Only the `main` branch exists locally (783 commits in total, mostly from the upstream open-source project), so work on other repositories or unpushed branches is not captured.

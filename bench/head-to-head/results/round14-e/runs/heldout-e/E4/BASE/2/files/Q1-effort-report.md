## Method

- **Commits:** commits authored by Elif Demir in the repository with an author date inside the period, in Istanbul time. Automated dependency-bot commits and upstream commits by other people are excluded. A commit made at 01:30 on 1 April (Istanbul time) falls in Q2 and is excluded.
- **Hours:** computed from the timestamps of exported AI-assistant sessions on this project. Time between consecutive activity is counted, but any gap longer than 30 minutes is treated as a break and not counted. Overlapping sessions are merged so that time is not counted twice, and activity after midnight on 1 April is cut off.
- **Excluded sessions:** one exact duplicate export, one session on an unrelated client project, and one session dated April 2026.

## Limitations

Session timestamps capture time spent working with the assistant, not offline work such as reading or thinking away from the tool. The hours figure is therefore a conservative estimate.

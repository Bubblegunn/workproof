## Method

- **Commits:** The count covers commits authored by Elif Demir on the p-limit repository whose timestamp falls within the period in Istanbul time. Automated dependency-bot commits and earlier upstream history are excluded. A sixth commit, made at 01:30 on 1 April Istanbul time, falls in Q2 and is not counted.
- **Working time:** This comes from timestamps in exported AI-assistant session logs for the p-limit project. Only the timestamps were used. Time between consecutive activity timestamps counts as work when the gap is 30 minutes or less. Longer gaps are treated as breaks and not counted.
- **Overlapping sessions:** Sessions that ran at the same time are counted once, not added together.
- **Excluded sessions:** A session for a different (client) project is not counted. A session dated in April is outside the period. A duplicate export of one session is counted once. A session that crosses midnight on 31 March is cut off at the end of the quarter.

Time spent without an assistant session, such as reading or offline work, is not captured, so the hours figure is likely a lower bound.

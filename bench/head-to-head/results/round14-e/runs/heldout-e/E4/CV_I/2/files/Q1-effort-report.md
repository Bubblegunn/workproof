## Method

- **Commits:** commits authored by Elif Demir on the p-limit repository with an author timestamp from 2026-01-01 00:00 to 2026-03-31 23:59 Istanbul time. Automated dependency-bot commits are excluded.
- **Hours:** taken from the timestamps of exported Claude Code sessions for the p-limit work. Time between consecutive activity events counts as active only when the gap is 30 minutes or less. Longer gaps are treated as breaks and not counted. Overlapping sessions are merged so the same minutes are not counted twice, and a duplicate export of one session was counted once.
- **Quarter boundaries:** time falling outside the quarter is clipped, so only the part of a late-night session that falls inside Q1 is counted.
- **Not included:** sessions on other projects, and work after 31 March 2026.
